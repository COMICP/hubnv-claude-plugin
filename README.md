# HubNV for Claude

Run your HubNV businesses from Claude. Ask for a status check across your websites, forms, storefront, requests and
NV Campaigns; read your latest leads, how your posts performed and a performance summary from HubNV's own figures; edit
a website, header and footer included (changes land in a "Claude changes" draft you review); draft Facebook and
Instagram posts, forms, a storefront and its listings, Ad Materials and NV Campaigns; see a preview of each before you
decide; connect your own domain; and schedule or publish when you say so. Anything that needs ad results or spending
goes to NV, HubNV's assistant, as a request you review and approve in HubNV.

## Connect

1. Add the plugin, then open its Connectors tab and connect **HubNV**. Or, in Claude, add a custom connector with the
   address `https://mcp.hubnv.com/mcp`.
2. Sign in with your HubNV login and select **Allow**.

Claude sees only the businesses you can open in HubNV, with your role's permissions. It asks before it publishes
anything, and it cannot delete your websites, forms, listings, posts or campaigns, boost a post, change billing, spend
on ads or change your team. Disconnect any time in
HubNV under Settings → Connected apps.

## Skills

- **hubnv-site-status**: a status check for one business.
- **hubnv-leads**: recent form responses.
- **hubnv-edit-site**: draft website edits, then publish on request.
- **hubnv-posts**: draft Facebook and Instagram posts, preview them, and schedule them when you agree.
- **hubnv-drafts**: new forms and questions, setting up a storefront and its listings, Ad Materials, NV Campaign drafts,
  connecting a domain, and handing ad work or spending to NV.
- **hubnv-daily-check**: a short check-in for a scheduled task.

Help: https://hubnv.com/documentation/account/connected-apps
